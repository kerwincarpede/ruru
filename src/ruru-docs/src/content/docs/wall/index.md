The wall is Ruru's one screen: a tile for every camera, each playing live.

- [Watch your cameras](/ruru/wall/watch/): maximise a tile, use the right-click menu, go full screen, and read what a tile says when its picture stops.
- [Arrange the wall](/ruru/wall/arrange/): drag and resize tiles, hide cameras, and fit the wall to the window.
- [Streams and playback](/ruru/wall/streams/): main and sub streams, WebRTC and MSE, and **Show stream info**.
