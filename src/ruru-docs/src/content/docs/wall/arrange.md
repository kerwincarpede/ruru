Make the front door big and the side gate small. Ruru remembers the layout for each Frigate server.

## Edit the layout

1. Choose **Edit layout** in the toolbar at the top right (move the pointer to show it), or right-click the wall and choose **Edit layout**.
2. Drag tiles to move them, and pull their corners to resize them. Tiles snap to a grid.
3. Choose **Done** when the wall looks right.

While you edit, the toolbar also shows the version of Ruru you have.

## Editing options

| Option | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Lock to 16:9** | Keeps a tile widescreen as you resize it. Untick it to resize freely. |
| **Fit to window** | On by default. Shrinks the wall so every tile fits the window without scrolling. It is also in the right-click menu. |
| **Reset to Frigate order** | Puts every camera back, in Frigate's order and size, and clears each tile's stream choice. |

Ruru remembers **Lock to 16:9**, **Fit to window** and **Show stream info** for next time.

## Hide and show cameras

Right-click a tile and choose **Hide** to take a camera off the wall. While you edit the layout, the toolbar has a **Show** button for each hidden camera, such as **Show Side gate**. Choose it to put the camera back.

## New cameras

A camera added to Frigate after you arranged the wall goes below your tiles, at the default size. Move it where you want it the next time you edit.
